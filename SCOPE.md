# Scope

This scope bounds the patent commitments made under the Community Specification License 1.0 (LICENSES/Community-Spec-1.0.txt) for the specification in `packages/spec` and the vocabularies in `packages/vocab`.

The specification defines data formats and vocabularies for recording the identity, design revision, composition, condition, custody and end-of-life recovery of physical assets in the built environment, and for exchanging those records:

- the material passport and event schemas, their JSON-LD context, and their validation rules;
- the event taxonomy, including two-party custody signatures and evidence references by hash;
- the joint type, material and cause vocabularies, and their mappings to external classifications;
- the records of revision and feature recovery performance derived from those events.

Out of scope: the implementation of any software that produces, stores, routes or displays these records, including the ledger, router, adapters and user interfaces in this repository, which are licensed separately under Apache-2.0.

Changes to this scope are not retroactive.
